Our perspective

Every Building Has a Story.

Every building has a history. Some stories are visible. Others are hidden within building materials, above ceilings, behind walls, or beneath flooring.

Our role is to uncover those stories, explain what they mean, and help you move forward with confidence.

The IES Method

Observe. Verify. Explain. Guide.

01

Observe

Careful field assessments grounded in experience, precision, and attention to detail.

02

Verify

Representative samples and accredited laboratory analysis when testing is appropriate.

03

Explain

Technical information translated into language that is practical and easy to understand.

04

Guide

Recommendations designed to support sound planning and informed decisions.
